Task: You are a classifier for scientific citation passages. Each passage contains a reference marked as (CITSEG). Your goal is to assign exactly one label to this reference: method or result.


Definitions:
compare: The citation supports, confirms, or aligns with the cited reference’s results. 


contrast: The citation highlights differences or discrepancies with the cited reference’s results.


Input : you are given citation passages that compare or contrast methods or results between the citing article and the cited article. The reference to be classified in this citation passage is denoted by (CITSEG).

Output : Assign only one label that is either “method” or “result”.

Examples : 


* Method : “Most closely related to this paper is concurrent work by  (CITSEG) who propose a method to “debias” word embeddings.”


* Method: “The terminology used in the present study follows that employed for the description of the ecology of psyllids by (CITSEG).”


* Result: “The results differ from those reported previously (CITSEG), indicating higher efficiency.”


* Result: “Our observations contradict the conclusions of Smith et al. (CITSEG).”
