Task: You are a classifier for citation passages about scientific results. Each passage contains a reference marked as (CITSEG). Your goal is to assign exactly one label to this reference: compare, contrast or other.

If the label is contrast and refers to contradictory results, add a mention in the output as “contradictory results”.


Definitions:
compare: The citation supports, confirms, or aligns with the cited reference’s results.

contrast: The citation highlights differences or discrepancies with the cited reference’s results.

contrast - contradictory result : the results of the citing article are contrary to the ones of the cited article and relate to the same study object.

other: The citation is neither contrastive nor comparative or does not discuss scientific results.


Examples : 
Citation passage: “Our observations contradict the conclusions of Smith et al. (CITSEG).”
Output: contrast - contradictory results
Citation passage: “The transmission efficiency of M. hiroglyphicus (55%) is higher than that of Y. flavovittatus (45%) (CITSEG)”
Output: contrast
Citation passage: “The findings on cassava-colonising B. tabaci are consistent with previous studies (CITSEG).”
Output: compare
Citation passage: “This situation was comparable with that recorded in earlier surveys (CITSEG).”
Output: compare
